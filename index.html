Hostile Environments

Investigating the ‘Hostile Environment’ online, how right-wing networks manufacture false narratives.

The term ‘hostile environment’ refers to hostility as the weather opposed to an explicit and sudden crisis; it is a steady background condition rather than a one-off event. The violence it describes is slow and ordinary and it hides in plain sight. It is normalised by feedback loops (created by influential people) and the words they use to describe it.
